`0x0BE830` is the 1-D lookup helper. The second family at 0x2F126-0x2F158 has
the same 2x2 shape and lands in the same workspace.

**Why this matters and what is NOT established.** The region touches
`transient_state_flag` and `FFFF7328`/`732C`/`7330`/`7334`, and `0x02F550`
nearby is the known transient-knock-inhibit writer. A coolant-indexed fraction
a little under 1.0, selected 2x2, feeding a transient workspace, has the SHAPE of
a wall-wetting / X-factor term -- which would bear directly on the cusp
tip-in work, where the stab-lean was concluded to be wall-wetting rather than
deliverable fuel.

That is a SHAPE ARGUMENT, not an identification. `0xFFFF72D0`, `0xFFFF72C8`,
`0xFFFF3158` and `gbr+101` are all unidentified. **Nothing here is named.**
Settling it means decoding 0x02F162 onward to the output store.

CAUTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* No table in this file is NAMED. Consumer RAM labels are themselves partly
  project guesses.
* FLAT is not permission to populate. `0xAD258` and `0xD2D48` are flat AND feed
  multipliers separately clamped to zero.
* 66 of 157 being inert is itself a result: most of the coolant-indexed surface
  in this ROM is switched off as shipped.
