HEADLINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The lift-to-cut delay is a per-gear x per-RPM-band uint16 count selected in
   the function at 0x03AF88 through pointer tables at 0x63B70-0x63BF0. The
   values live at 0xCBCA0-0xCBCF1, have NO definition, and are byte-identical to
   stock. Only the RPM band edges differ from stock (0xCC4EC 1800 -> 2250,
   0xCC4F0 2500 -> 3000, 0xCC4F4 4500 unchanged).
2. The timing drop seen at fuel resume is the task37 retard (corrections 74/79).
   It is NOT one-shot: it is re-armed on every pass while the overrun tier byte
   0xFFFF7C9A was 4 on the previous pass and rpm < [0xFFFF7C78], so it is HELD
   at the ECT table value (0xADE08, -24.96 deg warm) for the whole cut and then
   ramps out at +0.7 deg per call (0xD2C10). Table and rate are stock-identical.
3. Tier 4 IS on the dwell ladder (corrects item 79): the first test at 0x03AEBC
   is dwell >= [0xFFFF7C98], the top rung.
