(1) WHAT THE 20.19c MAF LIMIT 475 CHANGES

  0x01FE46 writes [63C4] ITSELF, and [63C4] is the capped value -- there is no separate "capped copy":
      [63C4] = min( [63C0] * [63F0] + [69F4] * 1.0 ,  MAF Limit 0x0C3100 )            (01FF48-01FF66)
  where [63C0] = ( max + min ) / 2 of the MAF samples taken since the previous call (01FEFA-01FF34):
  0x0203DC copies [40B4] into [63B4] and keeps the running max in [6420] and the running min in [6424];
  0x01FE46 snapshots them into [63B8] / [63BC] and restarts both at the current sample. [40B4] is written by
  0x004A2C as the definition "MAF Sensor Scaling" (data 0x0D8C9C, axis "MAF sensor") of word [4042]*5/65536 V,
  so [63C0], [63C4] are mass airflow in g/s (also: [63C0] feeds the "Mass Airflow" axis of the next table).
  [63F0] = "MAF Compensation (IAT)" = 1.0 at every cell in stock / 20.19c / 20.19e (only its +8 "Mass
  Airflow" axis was retuned); 0x0C3104 = 1.0.
  [69F4] is an additive term written by the state handlers under 0x0272E2 (GBR 0xFFFF69F0; identity not
  established); one writer (0x027408) stores a table value 0..0.786, another (0x027586) stores 0.0, five
  are not decoded.

  The cap applies to the averaged airflow and its result IS the airflow every reader of [63C4] uses
  (42 literal-pool readers found by find_writers --reads, a lower bound, 0x0252EA..0x0A9242, plus 0x020426).
  Inside 0x01FE46 the capped value becomes load (g/rev):
      [63E0] = min([63C4]*60/RPM,    Limit A)     [63DC] = min([63C4]*60/[679C], Limit A)
      [63E4] = min([63DC], Limit B(RPM) * 0x0AAE28([6364]) * 0x0AAE3C([6898]))      (the last two are 1.0)
  Limit A is the POOL WORD 0x020384 that the definition calls "Engine Load Limit A (Maximum)" (4.0 stock,
  8.0 tuned) and Limit B is "Engine Load Limit B Maximum (RPM)" (record 0x0AAE14: 3.9 stock, 7.99988 tuned;
  the tune doubled the record's scale field 0x0AAE20, 2^-14 -> 2^-13, and raised both data words 0x0C3608
  from 0xF99A to 0xFFFF).

  WHAT DIFFERS BETWEEN THE BINS, among everything this function reads (bytes, P6): the MAF limit 0x0C3100
  (300.0 -> 475.0; the only calibration scalar in 0x0C302D..0x0C315B that differs), the pool word 0x020384
  (Limit A), record 0x0AAE14's scale field and data (Limit B), the data and both axes of the two "Engine
  Load Compensation" maps 0x0AB004 / 0x0AB020, and the +8 axis of 0x0AAFE8 (its data stay 1.0). 20.19c and
  20.19e are identical in every one of them.

  In tuner's words:

    4.68 V (it tops out at 375.3 g/s at 5.0 V), so above that the ECU's airflow sat at 300 and load sat at
    300*60/RPM -- 3.0 g/rev at 6000 rpm -- and, separately, never above 3.9 g/rev (Limit B) / 4.0 (Limit A).

(3) THE [90A8] BLEND IS NUMERICALLY INERT IN EVERY AE5L600L IMAGE IN rom/
  [63F4] = (1 - [90A8]) * Cruise + [90A8] * NonCruise (020110-020136), with Cruise = record 0x0AB004 and
  NonCruise = record 0x0AB020. The two records have the same dimensions, typecode, scale and bias, and
  their axes (0x0C3BD8 / 0x0C3CD8, 100 bytes) and 11 x 14 uint8 data (0x0C3C3C / 0x0C3D3C) are byte-
  identical in stock, 20.19c and 20.19e -- and in all 20 images directly under rom/ (every tune rev; the
  three files in rom/reference/ are other ROMs and were not compared) (P6). So [63F4] is the Cruise value
  whatever [90A8] is: exactly
  at [90A8] = 0 and 1, within float rounding in between (one ulp in the cases emulated), NaN for a NaN
  [90A8]. Making the blend matter would take editing one of the two maps.
