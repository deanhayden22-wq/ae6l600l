9. THE MAX(mapterm, floor) STRUCTURE IS CONFIRMED FROM THE DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Independent confirmation of the decoded formula, from a direction that has
nothing to do with the transient term. At low load with fuel flowing and the map
term at ~0, the residual sits pinned at +0.1602 (FFB 12.67) across every ECT bin
from 150 to 206 degF. At WOT the map term is 0.3359 and the residual goes to ~0.

That is exactly what MAX(mapterm, floor) predicts: the FLOOR (0.160) wins at low
load, the MAP (0.336) wins at WOT, and the crossover is automatic. The formula
predicts a two-regime behaviour that the data shows.

CAUTION for anyone reusing that 0.160: it is NOT a single constant. Subtracting
it from low-load residuals to recover [7B8C] fails -- the resulting distribution
is bimodal (a second mode near residual +0.01), so there are at least two floor
regimes. The low-load window does NOT cleanly isolate the transient term.

10. THE MEASUREMENT PROBLEM IS SOLVED -- NO ROM PATCH NEEDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every residual figure in this file and its predecessor is computed against a
load PROXY (MAF*60/RPM) fed through a bilinear map interpolation. That proxy is
the largest remaining source of doubt.

It can be removed entirely. The SSM packing block at 0x0243C8-0x0245A4 already
packs the OL MAP RESULT itself into a logging slot:

  024554: D268  mov.l @(0x0246F8),r2
  024556: F428  fmov.s @r2,fr4
  024558: F6DC  fmov fr13,fr6
  02455A: C768  mova @(0x0246FC),r0
  02455C: FF08  fmov.s @r0,fr15
  02455E: 4D0B  jsr @r13

  024562: C112  mov.w r0,@(36,gbr)

    r2 = 0xFFFF798C (the map result), fr13 = 0.0 (bias, fldi0 at 0x0243D0),
    [0x0246FC] = 3.05176e-05 = 1/32768 (scale), GBR = 0xFFFF683C so gbr+36 is
    slot 0xFFFF6860. Packer 0x0BE5D8 computes
        word = clamp(round((value - bias)/scale), 0, 0xFFFF).

2-byte tag already used by every channel in logcfg.txt -- the unexercised 0xF4

12. CORRECTION TO SECTIONS 10-11 -- what the logging recipe does NOT do
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sections 10-11 were written as though logging 0xFF6860 would close the open
question of section 6. IT WILL NOT, and the claim is withdrawn.

  * 0xFF6860 measures [798C], the OL map result. The residual it enables,
        residual = 14.7/FFB - 1 - [798C]
    is still the SUM of every remaining addend:
        [73B4] + [7A08] + [7A14] + [7AF4] + [7B8C] - [7A44] + [7BCC] + [7BA8]
    plus the [7A20] contribution inside the map term and the multiplicative
    [7340]. It removes MEASUREMENT error. It does not do ATTRIBUTION.

  * Of the three packed channels, [7BA8] is already proven 0.0 (section 4), so
    it adds nothing but a self-test, and a weak one (the packer clamps negatives
    to 0, so a zero reading is consistent with several possibilities).

  * The terms that WOULD attribute -- [7B8C], [7A20], [7340] -- are NOT in the
    SSM packing block at 0x0243C8-0x0245A4. Reading them requires the 0xF4
    4-byte tag and therefore the SSM read patch, which sections 10-11 claimed
    was unnecessary.

WHAT SECTIONS 10-11 ARE STILL GOOD FOR, unchanged: logging 0xFF6860 retires the
