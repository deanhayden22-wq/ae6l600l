ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Item 89 traced a two-stage thermal-lag model tripping a flag at 930.0 and
inferred "an exhaust / catalyst temperature model driving OPEN-LOOP ENRICHMENT
for component protection", naming the consumer of byte[0xFFFF79FC] as the one
test that would settle it.

**Tested. The enrichment half is WRONG.** The flag feeds a maturation counter
inside a monitor function. Nothing in the chain reaches a fuelling path.

EVERY CONSUMER, ENUMERATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
byte[0xFFFF79FC] is gbr+88 with GBR = 0xFFFF79A4. Searched both access forms --
direct literal and GBR-relative `mov.b @(88,gbr)`:

    WRITE 0x036698, 0x0366AA    (the 930.0 set/clear, item 89)
    READ  0x0366E4, 0x03676C    <- the only two reads
    literal 0xFFFF79FC at 0x036A88, loaded at 0x03699C -- but as a BASE POINTER
      ([r14-48], [r14-44]), not a read of the flag byte

And the two filtered outputs the model produces:

    0xFFFF79A4  loaded at 0x03644C (this monitor's own GBR setup) and 0x024564
    0xFFFF79A8  loaded at 0x0457F4 (the trip calibrated OFF at 10000.0) and 0x024568

0x024564/0x024568 are the SSM getter pattern -- exposure for logging, not use.
**There is no fuel-path consumer anywhere in the image.**

A flag gating an incrementing counter, the counter compared against a calibrated
count, and a reset-and-return on reaching it, is the shape of a **fault
maturation** routine. It is not a control path.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR ITEM 89
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The model itself stands: a load x RPM base quantity, a knock-derived uplift, two
cascaded lag filters with separate rise/fall coefficients, a 930.0 trip. The
range (390..1138), the thermal-lag structure and the 930 limit are all still
consistent with an exhaust or catalyst temperature estimate.

What does NOT stand is "driving open-loop enrichment". That came from the
enclosing module's GBR being labelled `ol_enrichment_accum` -- a PROJECT GUESS --
plus the physical plausibility of the numbers. Neither is evidence, and the
consumer enumeration contradicts it.

Corrected reading: **a modelled temperature with a 930.0 over-temperature
monitor, whose only outputs are a maturation counter and two SSM-visible
values.** Whether it is exhaust, catalyst or something else is still not
established, and no name is asserted.
