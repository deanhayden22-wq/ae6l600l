ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The eight undefined coolant fractions found in slice 2 are the **decay rate** of
a single multiplicative accumulator with four staged latch-off thresholds. The
whole bank lives in the transient workspace, immediately adjacent to the
transient-knock-inhibit flag.

**They are not a wall-wetting lever at operating temperature.** Every one of the
eight curves is a CONSTANT 0.900 from 40 degC upward. The coolant axis only does
anything below ~40 degC. Item 87's shape argument is retired.

WHERE IT SITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GBR = `0xFFFF726C`, so the four flags are `0xFFFF726F`, `0xFFFF7270`,
`0xFFFF7271`, `0xFFFF7272`. `gbr+2` is **`0xFFFF726E`, the transient knock
inhibit flag** whose writer (`0x02F550`) is already traced in
`transient_knock_window_trace.txt`. This decay bank is in the same workspace
block, two bytes away.

`find_writers.py FFFF728C` returns exactly two writes, both inside this function
(`0x02F178` decay, `0x02F1EE` charge). Nothing else touches the accumulator.

Three things fall out:

1. **Above 40 degC all eight are a flat 0.900.** Normal operating coolant is
   80-100 degC. In that entire regime the coolant axis contributes nothing and
   every one of the eight tables is the same constant.
2. **Colder = SLOWER decay** (0.96/0.94 at -40..-30 degC vs 0.90 warm), so the
   accumulator and its four flags persist longer when cold. That is the
   signature of a cold-start hold, not a fuel-film term.
3. **The 2x2 selection is nearly moot.** `0xAC840` == `0xAC854` byte for byte,
   `0xAC890` == `0xAC8A4`, and all four of `0xAC818`/`868`/`82C`/`87C` are
   identical. Only the first family differs between its two pairs, and only
   below 40 degC.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE CUSP TIP-IN THREAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Item 87 flagged these as having the SHAPE of a wall-wetting / X-factor term,
which would have borne on the conclusion that the cusp stab-lean is wall-wetting
rather than deliverable fuel. **That is now ruled out for this path.** At the
80-100 degC the car actually runs at, these tables are a constant and cannot
explain any temperature-varying transient behaviour.

They remain a genuine cold-start lever, and they are still undefined in every
XML -- but they are not the thing.
